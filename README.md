# Case Study Project:
Show Stream is a subscription based streaming service platform catering to viewers interested in unique recorded Live Concerts, DJ Sets, and Theatrical performances.  The business follows a 12 month subscription ranging from Tier 1 premium, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cost plans.

Show Stream Executives would like a performance analysis from 2022 to 2025:

- Key KPI Metrics:
  - Subscriptions and Cancellations
  - Revenue and Cost
  - Tier service level adoption
- Key Insights for next steps for improving brand recognition and financial growth

# Key KPI Metric Insights:
- Show Stream demonstrates a rapid growth between the periods of 2022 to 2025
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) grew from $438 to $31,184
- Active subscriber base grew from 60 to 4281 users
  - Top 5 subscribers by state: CA at 895, TX at 732, FL at 325, AZ at 282, and TN at 207
- Annual revenue grew from $38,393 to $322,916, an increase of 741%
  - Lead by Tier 2 new subscriptions making up 49% of new sign ups
- Annual cost average remained steady over the 4 year period
  - Allowing for a healthy profit margin of 58.8%, to allow for:
    - Product Research and marketing campaigns
    - New content acquisition

# KPI Metric Concerns:

**Figures indicate that Show Stream has had healthy Financial Growth during this period.  However there are a few Key Metrics that show concern:**
- There is a spike in cancellations in 2025 reaching 942 cancellations
  - Primary reason by a combination of not enough content and switching to competitor
- Yearend new subscriptions percentages have dropped over previous years
  - 2022 indicating an average of 7.70% growth
  - 2023 indicating an average of 3.89% growth
  - 2024 indicating an average of 1.28% growth
  - 2025 indicating an average of 3.74% growth

# Recommended Next Steps:
- Address new subscriber growth decline:
  - Cancellations - users indicating not enough content and switching to competitor
    - Invest in new content acquisition
    - Conduct additional research as to why users are switching to competitor
- Tier 2 Subscriptions lead new sign ups and revenue
  - Optimize pricing for Tier 1 and Tier 3 cost plans
    - Introduce automated pause options in plans
    - Offer incentives for Tier 1 premium cost plan
    - Conduct additional research as to why users prefer Tier 2 over Tier 1
- Conducting additional research and analysis on churn tracking by studying user behavior
  - Track length of time users stay active on a plan
  - Conduct surveys on why users decide to cancel
  - Conduct surveys on Tier preferences
- Expand marketing in states that have the least amount of new subscribers
- Provide sign up incentives or discounts for states that have the most amount of new subscribers
- Develop campaigns for customer re-activations
